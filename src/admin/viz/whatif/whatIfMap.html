Read this with

  • Tidal and king-tide flooding are not included. The most frequent storm in the model is the 10-year storm, so seawall benefits are probably understated.
  • Rainfall flooding is modelled only in the C-8 and C-9 basins (outlined on the map). Elsewhere, inland benefits are probably understated.
  • Floor heights are mostly estimated, not surveyed.
